Hi! I’m Shagiththiah, a Computer Engineering undergraduate at the University of Peradeniya and a research intern at Crisis Lab, New Zealand. My interests span machine learning, connected systems, and computer systems performance.

I enjoy building software that connects research with practical problems—from flood severity classification for Sri Lanka to SmartIV, our intelligent IV drip monitoring system. My final-year research investigates cross-resource interference in heterogeneous ARM systems.
